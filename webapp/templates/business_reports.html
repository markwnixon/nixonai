Allocation Method Notes
Expense Allocation Methods
On Transaction Date
Recognizes the full expense on its transaction date. For manual allocation rows, the Period To date is used as the transaction anchor.
X Days Prior to TD
Recognizes the full expense a selected number of days before the transaction date. Example: driver pay with X = 7 recognizes payroll one week before the payment date.
Daily Spread
Spreads the amount evenly by day across the full selected report period or the manually entered allocation period.
Bill to Bill Prior Spread
For the selected expense account, uses actual ledger bill/payment dates and spreads each amount from the day after the previous same-account bill/payment through the current transaction date.
Port Trips
Allocates by the weekly count of port trips built from gate tickets. Paired gate tickets count as one trip; unpaired tickets count as one trip each.
Toll Transaction Weighted
Keeps the paid expense total from accounting but spreads each amount by actual toll transaction amounts in the matching allocation window.
Income Weighted
Allocates the amount based on each day or week's share of recognized income in the covered period.
Rolling Income Weighted
Allocates each expense over the prior four weeks, weighted by income in that four-week window.
Income Recognition
On Date Job Completed
Recognizes trucking income by the in-gate/return completion date and freight forwarding income by the in-gate date.
On Date Job Invoiced
Recognizes income by the invoice date when available.
